Nairobi · Lead Software Developer

Davis Kunyu Wamalwa is Lead Software Developer at D&V Technologies in Nairobi, from January 2025. The title is the work: software a company actually runs, shipped under his name and under the company.

That work is concrete. Payment bots take sales. A hotel system runs from the lobby through to the desk. Traffic tools sit with the night roads of Nairobi. The company site is dvtechnologies.xyz. Client sites he handles include Ndakaru Bricks and Construction and Bridan.

Before the lead role, from May to August 2024, he was an ICT intern at Posta, PCK GPO Nairobi. That season was networks, Wi-Fi, servers, backups, and support on the floor, the machines behind a public counter.

School ran through Friends' School Kamusinga, a diploma in Information Technology at Nairobi Technical Training Institute in 2020, and a BSc in Computer Science at the Catholic University of Eastern Africa in 2025, Second Class Honours, Upper Division, 62.39. With that degree sit Google Data Analytics, IBM Cybersecurity Analyst, and AWS Cloud Practitioner.

The public record is the GitHub account daviesqunyu. Repos update on their own below, and a hover on one that has a homepage opens that home page in a small window. Reach him by email, phone, or WhatsApp. The CV is on this site.
